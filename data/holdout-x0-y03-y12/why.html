is one of the four remaining holdout directions in the character sector conjugate to that containing the central cubic. It shares the same sextic and paired-sign symmetry, but shifts two even-coordinate powers into the middle odd coordinate. This makes it useful for testing whether a proposed construction addresses the full remaining rational Hodge structure or only one of its character components.

The ten remaining paired-sign directions separate into groups of six and four in conjugate characters. This dimension count does not assert that the structure is simple, or that a certificate for one monomial supplies all the others.

Passive-multiplier arguments from separated examples must therefore be checked against the actual field, not inferred from the numerator's factorization.
