The target
\[
A=X_0\cdot Y_0^3\cdot Y_1^2
\]
is one of the four remaining holdout directions in the character sector conjugate to that containing the central cubic. It shares the same sextic and paired-sign symmetry, but shifts two even-coordinate powers into the middle odd coordinate. This makes it useful for testing whether a proposed construction addresses the full remaining rational Hodge structure or only one of its character components.

Here a character records how a residue transforms under the diagonal automorphisms of the holdout; the volume determinant must be included along with the numerator. The ten remaining paired-sign directions separate into groups of six and four in conjugate characters. Together they belong to a rational Hodge substructure with Hodge numbers \((10,18,10)\) in types \((3,1),(2,2),(1,3)\). This dimension count does not assert that the structure is simple, or that a certificate for one monomial supplies all the others.

The fourth-degree factor \(X_0\cdot Y_0^3\) suggests starting with the initial binary pair and treating \(Y_1^2\) as a multiplier. The obstacle is that \(Y_1\) participates in both couplings of the sextic. Passive-multiplier arguments from separated examples must therefore be checked against the actual field, not inferred from the numerator's factorization.

Known finite quotients cover 48 of the 58 paired-sign-invariant degree-six directions, leaving this class outside their span. It is a specific residual target, not one for which geometric existence has already been extracted from that calculation.
