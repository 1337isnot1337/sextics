A finite-algebra certificate for holdout-q2

The entry point is Submission.certificate, proving the protected statement HodgeComputer.HasCertificate HodgeSubmission.F HodgeSubmission.A HodgeSubmission.q. The equation and target are unchanged. The construction uses strict certificates, so the quotient in the cleared divisibility identity is zero.

Write the protected variables as (x,a,c,y,b,d). Then

F=x^6+a^6+c^6+y^6+b^6+d^6+x^3ayb+a^3cbd, \qquad A=x^3a^2c^2y^3b^2,\qquad q=2.

Two endpoint targets

Choose k\in\mathbb C with k^2+k+1=0, and put P=x^3a^2y^3b^2 and \Delta_k=c^2-k^2d^2. We construct a certificate for each of P\Delta_k and P\Delta_{k^2}. The identity

Pc^2=\frac{2+k}{3}P(c^2-k^2d^2) +\frac{2+k^2}{3}P(c^2-kd^2)

then gives the prescribed target. The proofs use complex coefficients directly; no rational descent is needed by the acceptance contract.

The scaled endpoint reflections motivate this decomposition, but the Lean proof does not assume a geometric reflection theorem or a support-to-certificate theorem. It constructs the required algebraic witnesses.

Cubic reduction

Let

\alpha=y/x,\quad\beta=b/a,\qquad U=x^2,\quad V=a^2,\quad S=c^2+k^2d^2,\quad T=kcd.

Use four independent coefficient parameters \mathsf a,\mathsf b,\mathsf c,\mathsf d and the cubic

G=S^3-3ST^2+\mathsf aU^3+\mathsf bV^3 +\mathsf cU^2V+\mathsf dV^2T.

The coefficient substitution is

(\mathsf a,\mathsf b,\mathsf c,\mathsf d) =(1+\alpha^6,1+\beta^6,\alpha\beta,\beta k^2).

It gives exactly F. At (\alpha,\beta)=(k^2,k), these four coefficients are (2,2,1,1). This is the common good fiber for the construction.

Constructing lines and serving every quadratic source

Work in the localization of \mathbb Q[\mathsf a,\mathsf b,\mathsf c,\mathsf d] at the polynomials nonzero at (2,2,1,1). A line has equations S=eU+fV, T=gU+hV. With f=re, elimination gives an explicit degree-27 polynomial in r, whose leading coefficient is 729\mathsf a^{11}. The proof normalizes this polynomial to be monic and forms its finite free AdjoinRoot algebra.

The line coordinates are rational expressions in the root. Their denominator units are proved by exact polynomial Bezout identities on the good fiber and specialization of multiplication matrices. Universal polynomial identities then prove all four line equations over the unspecialized coefficient ring. No irreducibility or squarefreeness assumption is made about the eliminant.

In local coordinates \ell=S-eU-fV, m=T-gU-hV, write G=\ell Q_1+mQ_2. The elementary field

\frac1\ell\left((Q_2)_V\partial_U-(Q_2)_U\partial_V\right)

has zero divergence and sends G to (Q_1)_U(Q_2)_V-(Q_1)_V(Q_2)_U. The local derivatives hold \ell,m fixed. LineContact.lean proves the transported polynomial source, tangency, and divergence identities.

The four gradient sources and six coefficient functionals of the line source form an invertible ten-by-ten matrix on the good fiber, in the quadratic basis

U^2,UV,US,UT,V^2,VS,VT,S^2,ST,T^2.

The exact rational matrix inverse and the reductions giving its entries are checked by Lean. Specialization proves that the generic determinant is a unit. Thus the same finite line algebra serves every quadratic source.

The regular representation, determinant, and adjugate clear the line pole:

D_0=\det(SI-UM_e-VM_f).

For any quadratic source h, the construction gives numerators P_i of degree 27 such that

\sum_iP_iG_i=D_0h,\qquad D_0\sum_i\partial_iP_i-\sum_iP_i\partial_iD_0=0.

The proof reuses the accepted determinant/adjugate calculus rather than expanding this degree-27 determinant.

A small exact quintic reduction

The preliminary 56-by-56 Jacobian inversion is replaced by four explicit cubic polynomials in QuinticReduction.lean. Put

R=27\mathsf a^2\mathsf b+4\mathsf c^3, \qquad H=R(R^4-2916\mathsf a^8\mathsf d^6).

The displayed numerators M_i satisfy \sum_iM_iG_i=HU^3V^2, proved by polynomial arithmetic. At the good fiber, H=515198970880\ne0, so N_i=M_i/H is defined in the coefficient localization. Let h=\operatorname{div}N, which is quadratic.

Apply the quadratic solver to h, and write W_1=P/D_0. With the four-variable Euler field E, define

W_2=\frac{N+GW_1}{2}-\frac{hE}{6}.

The Euler identities E(G)=3G, E(h)=2h, and \operatorname{div}E=4 give

2W_2(G)=U^3V^2,\qquad\operatorname{div}W_2=0.

The actual cleared representation has denominator 6D_0 and numerators 3(D_0N_i+GP_i)-D_0hX_i, of degree 30. Multiplication by the passive scalar \alpha^3\beta^2/2 gives the required downstairs source.

Lifting and homogeneous clearing

For normalized T=kcd, the four horizontal fields are

\begin{aligned} C_U&=\frac{\Delta_k}{x}\partial_x+ \frac{\Delta_k y}{x^2}\partial_y,& C_V&=\frac{\Delta_k}{a}\partial_a+ \frac{\Delta_k b}{a^2}\partial_b,\\ C_S&=c\partial_c-d\partial_d,& C_T&=-2kd\partial_c+2k^2c\partial_d. \end{aligned}

They have zero divergence, annihilate both passive ratios, and act on (U,V,S,T) as 2\Delta_k times the respective coordinate derivatives. The chain-rule proofs use derivations on the actual fraction field of the six-variable polynomial ring. Derivatives of the passive ratios are included.

The coefficient map between the two localizations is constructed, with its specialization law proved. A simultaneous homogeneous clearing of the finitely many coefficients produces polynomial representatives Q,P_i. If the extra clearing exponent is n, their degrees are 2(n+27) and 2(n+30). The additional coordinate denominator is x^2a^2. After applying the displayed horizontal fields, the final numerator degrees exceed the denominator degree by seven, as required for q=2. Common-clearing-factor derivative terms cancel in the divergence identity.

Denominator legality and final combination

The exact inverse of (e+f)^3+5 in the good-fiber line algebra gives an explicit matrix Bezout identity. It proves that

\det(c^2I-M_{e+f})\quad\hbox{and}\quad c^6+5

are relatively prime over \mathbb C. On the specialization (x,a,y,b,d)=(1,1,k^2,k,0), the sextic is exactly c^6+5, and the cleared denominator is a nonzero scalar times that norm polynomial. The sextic is monic in c, so the specialization theorem lifts relative primality to the full polynomial ring. It also proves denominator nonvanishing.

Finally, the two endpoint certificates are combined over a product denominator. Relative primality, homogeneity, and the full cleared identity are preserved. Solution.lean binds the resulting theorem to the generated protected target.
