A certificate for one-reflection-y2-sector from a Fermat cover and a moving conic

This submission proves the exact existential HodgeComputer.HasCertificate statement for the protected instance one-reflection-y2-sector, with (F,q,A) unchanged:

F=X_0^6+X_1^6+X_2^6+Y_0^6+Y_1^6+Y_2^6+X_0^3X_1Y_0Y_1+X_1^3X_2Y_0Y_1,\qquad A=X_0^3Y_0Y_1Y_2,\qquad q=1 .

Nonconstructive existence was already known from the reflection theorem of the book (the sextic is even in Y_2 and the target is odd in it). What is supplied here is a finite algebraic existence proof checked by Lean, obtained by adapting the accepted holdout solution of fqlx (hodge-computer/sextics PR #128).

1. The same cover, a specialised quotient

Both couplings of F contain the factor Y_0Y_1. Adjoin T with T^6=Y_0^6+Y_2^6 and put \alpha=Y_0/T, \beta=Y_2/T, y=Y_1, so that \alpha^6+\beta^6=1. On this cover

G=X_0^6+X_1^6+X_2^6+y^6+T^6+\alpha\,yT\,(X_0^3X_1+X_1^3X_2),

which is invariant under the interchange y\leftrightarrow T. With S=y^2+T^2, P=yT the quotient is

H=X_0^6+X_1^6+X_2^6+\alpha P\,(X_0^3X_1+X_1^3X_2)+S^3-3SP^2,

that is, the holdout quotient H(\alpha,\beta) of PR #128 with both coupling coefficients equal to \alpha. In the Lean sources this is expressed by using weightedHypersurface alpha alpha and commonCoverPolynomial alpha alpha on the quotient and cover sides; the coefficient field is still the function field of the Fermat curve, because the cover coordinate \beta is needed for Y_2. The modules handling the generic conic family, the étale coefficient algebra, the polynomial normal vector, the polynomial integration of the residue equation, finite trace descent, legal clearing and homogeneous extraction are reused with their original attribution.

2. The conic seed on the equal-coefficient quotient

fqlx's conic (equations (8)–(10) of the holdout write-up) lies on H(\alpha_c,\beta_c) for every value of its parameter r; the degree-23 polynomial there only enforces the Fermat relation between the two coefficients. Here the two coefficients must coincide instead: \beta_c=\alpha_c is equivalent to k_c^2=1-r, and with q_c^2=M/(1-r)^3 the relation q_c^4=R becomes the integer polynomial

g(r)=r^8+7r^7-58r^6+149r^5-178r^4+115r^3-44r^2+10r-1 ,

which has a root in (-13,-12) by the intermediate value theorem (g(-13)=35869443, g(-12)=-35000185). For r<-12 the auxiliary quantities m,R,M,a,b are positive, 1-r is positive, and both Jacobian polynomials g_{\rm even},g_{\rm odd} of the accepted proof are negative, each being minus a polynomial in -12-r with positive coefficients. Thus the seed is nonsingular exactly as in PR #128 (gEven_neg, gOdd_neg, seedJacobian_nonsingular). The cover coordinate \beta at the seed is any sixth root of 1-\alpha_c^6 (Seed.baseBeta), which puts the seed on the Fermat curve (Seed.base_relation); the curve identity on H(\alpha_c,\alpha_c) is Seed.curve, obtained from fqlx's identity through Seed.beta_eq_alpha.

3. Source and deck trace for the target

With A=X_0^3Y_0Y_1Y_2 the pulled-back density, symmetrised under y\leftrightarrow T and divided by 2(y^2-T^2), is the weighted-degree-five source

B=\frac{\beta X_0^3P}{2\alpha^4},\qquad J_c\,B=A-\bigl(X_0^3Y_0Y_1^3Y_2\bigr)\,T^{-2},\qquad J_c=\frac{2\alpha^5(T^2-y^2)}{T}.

The reflected companion is A(y/T)^2; under the deck automorphism T\mapsto\zeta T fixing the physical field it is a \zeta^{-2}-eigenvector, so for a primitive cube root \zeta its normalized trace vanishes (commonCover_trace_inverse_square). The closure then recovers the exact source A and the remaining steps are the accepted ones. The scalar identities are cover_source_identity and cover_normal_source_identity; their bindings to the residue fields are cover_source_binding and cover_normal_source_binding.
