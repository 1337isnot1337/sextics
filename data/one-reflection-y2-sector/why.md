This is the sextic of [One reflection · residual sector](#case/one-reflection), with a target odd in \(Y_2\) and symmetric in \(Y_0,Y_1\). The \(Y_2\) reflection predicts existence by [Chapter 3, §3.3](#technical-information/geometric-support/33-reflection-quotients). The companion target's explicit binary-cubic construction, however, uses the densities \(Y_0^3+Y_1^3\) and \(Y_0^3-Y_1^3\); the transverse target lies outside both density sectors, and passive multipliers do not remove that obstruction.

The [finite-cover construction](#technical-information/explicit-constructions/27-creating-a-reflection-on-a-finite-cover) gives a different realization of the predicted support. Adjoining \(T^6=Y_0^6+Y_2^6\) exposes an exchange between \(Y_1\) and \(T\). Both couplings of this sextic contain \(Y_0Y_1\), so its weighted quotient has equal coupling coefficients. `DiamondJM` adapts `fqlx`'s moving-conic family by finding a nonsingular seed on that equal-coefficient locus; the Fermat base coordinate for \(Y_2\) remains separate. A polynomial source on the quotient then recovers the prescribed target after an inverse-square correction vanishes under deck trace. This supplies a checked finite-algebraic existence proof, although no expanded divisor is supplied. The instance distinguishes geometric existence, the target range of a particular density map, and the concrete incidence calculations needed to turn another quotient into a certificate.
