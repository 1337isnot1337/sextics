This is the sextic of [One reflection · residual sector](#case/one-reflection), with a target odd in \(Y_2\) and symmetric in \(Y_0,Y_1\). **Nonconstructive existence is known** from the reflection quotient theorem in [Chapter 3, §3.3](#technical-information/geometric-support/33-reflection-quotients): the \(Y_2\) reflection serves this target. The challenge is to turn that support argument into a concrete certificate, or to formalize the existence proof itself. The explicit binary-cubic construction for the companion target uses the densities \(Y_0^3+Y_1^3\) and \(Y_0^3-Y_1^3\); this target is outside the density sectors of both corresponding maps. Multiplying those fields by passive functions does not change that fact. Thus knowing a certificate on the same variety does not automatically provide one here. The instance tests whether the remaining reflection quotient admits an equally effective algebraic construction, or requires a different realization of its Fano geometry.
