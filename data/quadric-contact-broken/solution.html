A finite-algebra contact certificate for quadric-contact-broken

The theorem entry point is Submission.certificate, with the exact target HodgeComputer.HasCertificate HodgeSubmission.F HodgeSubmission.A HodgeSubmission.q. The construction gives a strict certificate: the residual polynomial in the cleared identity is zero. The line over a finite coefficient algebra, the invertible source matrix, and denominator legality are constructed within the proof.

Cubic reduction

Write the protected coordinates as (x,a,c,y,b,d) and put

\alpha=b/a,\quad\beta=d/c,\qquad S=2(x^2+y^2),\quad T=4xy,\quad B=a^2,\quad C=c^2.

Let

G=2S^3+ST^2+S(pB^2+qBC+sC^2)+kTBC+uB^3+vC^3,

where

(p,q,s,k,u,v)= \left(40\alpha^2,40\alpha\beta,40\beta^2, \tfrac{20}{7}\alpha(1+2\beta^2),16(1+\alpha^6),16(1+\beta^6)\right).

After this substitution, the exact composition is G = 16F. This identity, the source composition BT = 4a²xy, and the derivative identities are supplied by the Lean modules.

The line and its finite coefficient algebra

Work over the localization O of ℚ[α,β] at all polynomials nonzero at (1,2). Evaluation at that point is a total ring homomorphism O → ℚ; every element with nonzero evaluation is a unit.

A line is written

S=\xi B+\eta C,\qquad T=\zeta B+\omega C.

Set η=rξ, ω=rζ+(v−ur³)/(kr). Eliminating ζ leaves a quadratic and a cubic in ξ. Their resultant is k¹²r⁹ times an explicit degree-27 polynomial. The leading coefficient of that polynomial is u¹⁰v, a unit in O. Normalize it to a monic polynomial P and form the finite free algebra E=O[r]/(P), with basis 1,r,…,r²⁶.

The resultant and subresultant identities are proved as universal commutative ring identities. Exact rational polynomial Bézout identities at (α,β)=(1,2) show that r, the quadratic leading coefficient, and the relevant linear subresultant coefficient are units in the specialized algebra. Their multiplication matrices specialize functorially. Nonzero specialized determinants therefore lift these units to E. The subresultant formula then constructs ξ, and the chart constructs η,ζ,ω. All four coefficients of the restriction of G to this line are proved zero. Neither irreducibility nor squarefreeness of P is needed.

A strict cubic certificate

Put

L=S-\xi B-\eta C,
U=\eta T+(2\xi\omega+\eta\zeta+k)B+3\eta\omega C, \qquad V=\xi T+3\xi\zeta B+(\xi\omega+2\eta\zeta+k)C.

The polynomial vector (ξU−ηV, ζU−ωV, U, −V) has zero divergence, is tangent to L, and sends G to LJ for a quadratic polynomial J. The monomials spanning J, in order, are SB, SC, T², TB, TC, B², BC, C².

Take the first eight power-basis coordinates of these eight source coefficients. The resulting square matrix has an explicitly supplied rational inverse on the good fiber. Lean checks both the source reductions modulo P and the matrix inverse. This proves that its generic determinant is a unit in O. The corresponding inverse column defines an O-linear functional that sends J to TB when applied to its coefficients.

Apply the regular representation of E and clear the matrix pencil L by its adjugate. The scalar denominator is its determinant D₀; the four scalar numerators are obtained by the functional applied to the numerator matrices times that adjugate. Differentiating the adjugate identity proves

\sum_i N_{0,i}\partial_iG=D_0BT,\qquad D_0\sum_i\partial_iN_{0,i}=\sum_iN_{0,i}\partial_iD_0.

The denominator and all four numerators are homogeneous of degree 27. The denominator is nonzero because the pencil evaluates to the identity at (S,T,B,C)=(1,0,0,0).

Homogeneous clearing and the sextic lift

Only finitely many coefficients occur. Clear their denominators simultaneously in O, then homogenize their substitution α=b/a, β=d/c using a common power of ac. This produces polynomials Q,P_S,P_T,P_B,P_C homogeneous of a common degree h=2(n+27) for some natural number n. In the fraction field, they are the substituted D₀,N₀ multiplied by one polynomial clearing factor H. On b=a,d=2c, this factor is κ(ac)^n for a rational scalar κ≠0.

Let δ=x²−y². The final denominator and numerators, in protected order, are

D=a^2c^2Q,
N=\bigl( a^2c^2(xP_S-yP_T),\; 2\delta ac^2P_B,\; 2\delta ca^2P_C,\; a^2c^2(-yP_S+xP_T),\; 2\delta bc^2P_B,\; 2\delta da^2P_C \bigr).

The horizontal derivations are x∂x−y∂y, −y∂x+x∂y, a∂a+b∂b, and c∂c+d∂d. They are extended to the actual fraction field and proved to kill the parameter coefficient ring. Their chain rule has scales 4δ,4δ,2a²,2c². Together with G=16F, this makes the rational field N/D send F to (δ/4)BT=δa²xy=A. In each divergence cross term the derivative of the common clearing factor cancels. These facts prove the exact strict equations directional F N = A*D and divergenceNumerator D N = 0. The denominator is homogeneous of degree 2n+58, and every numerator is homogeneous of degree 2n+59, as required for q=1. These statements also cover a component that vanishes identically.

Denominator legality

Specialize (a,c,y,b,d)=(1,1,0,1,2), leaving x free. The protected sextic becomes x⁶+35x²+67. The final denominator becomes κ times

\det(2x^2I-M_{\xi+\eta}).

An exact polynomial Bézout identity in the specialized degree-27 quotient proves that M³+140M+536I is invertible for M=M_{ξ+η}. A matrix polynomial Bézout identity gives relative primality of the displayed determinant and x⁶+35x²+67, using (2x²)³+140(2x²)+536=8(x⁶+35x²+67). The protected sextic is monic in x, so the repository's specialization theorem lifts this relative primality to the full polynomial ring. In particular, the final denominator is nonzero.

Finally, coefficient extension from ℚ to ℂ preserves the polynomial identities and homogeneity. Solution.lean binds the resulting sextic and numerator to the exact protected target.
